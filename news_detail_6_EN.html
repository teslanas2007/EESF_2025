🌍 The Second Consortium Meeting of the Erasmus+ CBHE EESF Project – Empowering Educators for Sustainable Future: Enhancing Teacher Training Capacity for Ukraine's Recovery – Has Started in Uzhhorod!

On 5-6 November 2026, Uzhhorod National University is hosting the partners of the international Erasmus+ CBHE EESF project. The two-day meeting brings together representatives of partner universities and organisations to discuss current issues in Education for Sustainable Development (ESD), coordinate project activities, and identify the next steps in their joint work.

📚 The first day of the meeting is dedicated to the international round table “Education for Sustainable Development: Implementation Specifics in Ukraine”.

Participants are discussing the challenges and opportunities of integrating ESD principles into Ukraine's education system, the role of universities in the country's recovery, the development of international partnerships, and practical approaches to creating resilient, safe, and inclusive learning environments.

Particular attention is being paid to the experience of Ukrainian higher education institutions in implementing ESD, strengthening institutional resilience during wartime, and fostering cooperation between universities, communities, and international partners.

🎓 During the round table, experts from Dragomanov Ukrainian State University, Hryhorii Skovoroda University in Pereiaslav, the National University of Ostroh Academy, Uzhhorod National University, and the Association of Sustainable Development Experts are presenting their experience, achievements, and perspectives.

🤝 The second day of the meeting will focus on coordinating project implementation and reviewing progress across the work packages. Partners will discuss the development and implementation of ESD courses for pre-service and in-service teachers, professional development opportunities for university educators and administrative staff, the establishment of university sustainability laboratories, the development of gender equality strategies, and the dissemination of project results.

Special attention will also be given to coordinating project timelines, ensuring the quality of project activities, and identifying shared priorities for the next stages of EESF implementation.

The second consortium meeting provides an opportunity not only to assess current progress but also to strengthen cooperation among partners, exchange experience, and develop a shared vision for the project's future.

🌱 Together, we are working to make Education for Sustainable Development an integral part of teacher education, university development, and Ukraine's recovery!
