The First Consortium Meeting of the Erasmus+ CBHE №101237551 EESF Project Took Place

The first consortium meeting of the Erasmus+ CBHE №101237551 EESF project “Empowering Educators for Sustainable Future: Enhancing Teacher Training Capacity for Ukraine’s Recovery” brought together project partners to review progress and discuss upcoming activities.

During the meeting, participants reflected on the work already completed and outlined key priorities for the next phase of project implementation. Particular attention was paid to project coordination, reporting procedures, dissemination activities, and the responsibilities of consortium members.

Project Coordinator Tetiana Matusevych presented an overview of the achievements accomplished so far and highlighted the main tasks to be undertaken by Ukrainian and European partners. Communication and visibility activities, reporting requirements, and future collaborative actions were also discussed.

The meeting provided an important opportunity to strengthen cooperation among project partners and ensure effective implementation of activities aimed at enhancing teacher education capacity for sustainable development and Ukraine’s recovery.
